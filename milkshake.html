McDonald Milkshake

Brief history of the McDonald Milkshake.

Originally released in 1954 as a simple fruit milkshake by franchise owner Ray Kroc. Then in 1975, McDonald released a limited time item called the Shamerock Shake. The "Shamrock Shake" which was meant to be a limited time item for St. Patricks Day. To market the new product, McDonald's used the uncle of Grimace, a green monster-like character to attract young customers. Similiar to the new revamped purple grimace shake which came out last year. Except this milkshake was green. This obviously worked out well as both Grimace-themed shakes ended up selling incredibly well across all franchises.

Ingredients

  • 1 Cup Raspberries or 1⁄4 Cup Raspberrie Jam
  • 1 Tsp Honey (not neccessary if you are using jam)
  • 1 Cup Vanilla Ice Cream
  • 1 Cup Milk (Your choice of milk)
  • 1⁄2 Cup Frozen Blueberries
  • 1⁄2 Cup Frozen Strawberries

Optional toppings

  • Whipped cream
  • Sprinkles

Recipe

(2 Servings)

  1. Crush raspberries in a bowl and mix with honey. (Skip if you are using jam instead).
  2. Combine ice cream, frozen blueberries, strawberries, raspberry paste/jam into blender.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Add toppings of your choice.
