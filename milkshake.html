McDonald Milkshake

Brief history of the McDonald Milkshake

Originally released in 1954 as a simple fruit milkshake by franchise owner Ray Kroc. Then in 1975, McDonald released a limited time item called the Shamrock Shake for St. Patrick's Day. To market the new product, McDonald's used the uncle of Grimace, a green monster-like character to attract young customers. Similar to the new revamped purple grimace shake which came out last year.

Ingredients

  • 1 Cup Raspberries or 1⁄4 Cup Raspberry Jam
  • 1 Tsp Honey (not necessary if using jam)
  • 1 Cup Vanilla Ice Cream
  • 1 Cup Milk (Your choice of milk)
  • 1⁄2 Cup Frozen Blueberries
  • 1⁄2 Cup Frozen Strawberries

Optional toppings

  • Whipped cream
  • Sprinkles

Recipe (2 Servings)

  1. Crush raspberries in a bowl and mix with honey (skip if using jam).
  2. Combine ice cream, frozen blueberries, strawberries, and raspberry paste/jam into blender.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Add toppings of your choice.
