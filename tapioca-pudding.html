Baked & Cripsy Tapioca Pudding

Origin

Tapioca pudding or sago is a Hong Kong style café dessert. This dessert is iconic for its diamond-like patterned crust, similar to the ones you would find on Chinese pineapple buns. This dish istypically sold in cafes as a small custard or as a large cake at the end of a Dim Sum meal. Hong Kong style tapioca pudding is generally made with a base, some sort of filling and a crisp crust. It is said that this dish was inspired by Malaysian and Singaporean culture.

About this Recipe

  • Estimated prep time: ~30 minutes. (Does not include time to refrigerate)
  • Estimated cooking time: ~45 minutes.
  • Total time: ~1 hour 15 minutes.
  • 8 Servings.

Ingredients

Topping

  • 31⁄2 Tsp of unsalted butter.
  • 1⁄4 Cup granulated sugar.
  • 3⁄4 Cup cake flour.
  • 1 Egg yolk
  • 1⁄4 Tsp baking powder.
  • 1⁄8 Tsp Baking soda.

Sesame Paste

  • 31⁄3 Tbsp of black sesame powder.
  • 1 Tbsp of unsalted butter.
  • 2 Tbsp of milk.
  • 1⁄3Cup granulated sugar.

Tapioca Custard

  • 1⁄2Cup uncooked tapioca pearls.
  • 250ml of Milk.
  • 5 Tbsp of granulated sugar.
  • 11⁄2 Tbsp of unsalted butter.
  • 2 Egg yolks
  • 2 Eggs
  • 212 Tbsp of corn starch.
  • 1 Tsp of vanilla extract.

Prep for topping

  1. Combine cake flour with baking soda, baking powder in bowl. Mix well.
  2. In another bowl, mix butter with sugar. For this step you could use an electric mixer or a whisk.
  3. Add egg yolk into butter & sugar mixture. Mix well.
  4. Now combine your dry ingredients and wet ingredients together.
  5. Mix until no flour powder is visable. Then shape into a ball.
  6. Place the ball inbetween two pieces of plastic wrap. Then roll it out to form one large flat circle. Roughly 0.5cm thick.
  7. Refirgerate for 45 Minutes.

Recipe for sesame paste.

  1. Using a non-stick pan, on low-medium heat add your butter, black sesame powder and sugar.
  2. Cook until it becomes a fine paste.
  3. Add your milk into the pan and continue cooking for ~5 min. Or until your milk combines with the paste. After it has combined, remove heat and move the pan to the side.

Recipe for Tapioca Custard

  1. Fill up a small pot roughly halfway with water and boil.
  2. After it has started boiling, add your tapioca pearls and continue boiling for one minute. Then cover the pot and turn off the heat. Let the pearls steam for about 15 minutes. The pearls should turn transparent when they are complete.
  3. When the pearls are fully cooked, drain the water into colander and rinse pearls with cold water.
  4. Transfer the pearls into a seperate bowl and submerge in water. We will come back to the pearls later.
  5. In a mixing bowl, combine your eggs and egg yolk with 2 tbsp of sugar. Mix well until the mixture is smooth.
  6. Now add 212 tbsp of corn starch to the mixture and whisk everything together.
  7. In another sauce pan, pour 250ml of milk and your remaining sugar. After it has begun boiling, pour roughly a quarter of it into the egg mixture. Then mix the egg mixture until it is smooth.
  8. Now pour all of the egg mixture back into the sauce pan. Bring heat to medium-high or medium to avoid burning the milk.
  9. Whisk the mixture until it thickens and is smooth. Then turn off the heat.
  10. Add vanilla extract and butter to the mixture and mix until everything fully combines.
  11. Now prepare a pie pan and spread half of the custard mixture on the bottom.
  12. Spread your black sesame paste above the bottom layer.
  13. At last, cover the paste with the remaining tapioca custard. Ideally, the paste should be fully covered on all edges of the pan.
  14. Retrieve your crispy topping pastry from the fridge and unwrap the plastic. Then place it over your custard. You may also want to score the top of your pastry if you are going for a specific look. I.e. diamond/checker pattern.
  15. Crack open a egg and paste the top with a brush.
  16. Bake for 200°C or 400°F for 20 minutes. Let the custard cool for 5-10 minutes before serving.
