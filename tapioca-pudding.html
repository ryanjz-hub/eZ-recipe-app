Baked & Crispy Tapioca Pudding

Origin

Tapioca pudding or sago is a Hong Kong style café dessert. This dessert is iconic for its diamond-like patterned crust, similar to the ones you would find on Chinese pineapple buns. This dish is typically sold in cafes as a small custard or as a large cake at the end of a Dim Sum meal. Hong Kong style tapioca pudding is generally made with a base, some sort of filling and a crisp crust. It is said that this dish was inspired by Malaysian and Singaporean culture.

About this Recipe

  • Estimated prep time: ~30 minutes (does not include refrigeration time).
  • Estimated cooking time: ~45 minutes.
  • Total time: ~1 hour 15 minutes.
  • 8 Servings.

Ingredients

Topping

  • 31⁄2 Tsp unsalted butter
  • 1⁄4 Cup granulated sugar
  • 3⁄4 Cup cake flour
  • 1 Egg yolk
  • 1⁄4 Tsp baking powder & 1⁄8 Tsp baking soda

Sesame Paste

  • 31⁄3 Tbsp black sesame powder
  • 1 Tbsp unsalted butter & 2 Tbsp milk
  • 1⁄3 Cup granulated sugar

Tapioca Custard

  • 1⁄2 Cup uncooked tapioca pearls
  • 250ml Milk & 5 Tbsp granulated sugar
  • 11⁄2 Tbsp unsalted butter & 1 Tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 Eggs + 2 Egg yolks
  • 21⁄2 Tbsp corn starch

Instructions

  1. Topping: Mix dry ingredients; cream butter and sugar with egg yolk. Combine to form a dough ball, flatten to 0.5cm between plastic wrap, and refrigerate 45 minutes.
  2. Sesame Paste: Melt butter with black sesame powder and sugar over low heat. Stir in milk until smooth paste forms, then cool.
  3. Custard: Boil tapioca pearls for 1 minute, cover and steam 15 minutes until clear, then drain and rinse. Whisk eggs, yolks, sugar, and cornstarch. Heat milk and temper into egg mixture. Cook on medium-high until thick. Stir in butter, vanilla, and cooked pearls.
  4. Assembly & Bake: Layer half the custard in a pie dish, spread sesame paste, and cover with remaining custard. Top with chilled pastry dough, score diamond pattern, brush with egg wash, and bake at 200°C (400°F) for 20 minutes.
