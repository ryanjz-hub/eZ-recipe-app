About

This site is dedicated to showcasing my favourite dishes from different cuisines around the world. The cuisines I'll specifically target for my site are Western fast food and dishes from Southeast China.

I chose to create a section for Western fast food because it is one of the biggest sectors in the food industry. I also think that fast food is extremely relatable for most people as we've all probably tried it at least once. Growing up, fast food has always been one of my favourite go-to comfort food options. Specifically, I enjoy its simplicity and convenience. Being easily purchasable and enjoyed, even during a time crunch.

As for Southeast Chinese food, I picked this cuisine because it plays a big role in my daily diet and flavour preferences. Cantonese cuisine to me means a balance of fresh ingredients with subtle spices acting in harmony to balance each other out. It has become the basis and foundation of my flavour pallet. Due to this, I find myself attracted to other dishes with the same principles. Balanced while emphasizing the freshness of ingredients.

For the recipes that are not originally my own, I will be citing the creator and the site I found it on. This will be the case for most of the recipes as I don't often cook. I simply just enjoy eating flavourful dishes like the rest of us.

My motivation for creating a site dedicated to sharing recipes was because I wanted to learn how to cook. The main goal was to research different cooking techniques and to learn the history of my favourite recipes.
