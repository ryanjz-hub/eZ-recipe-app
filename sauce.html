Big Mac Sauce

McDonald's unique flavor

“Get a taste of this creamy, zesty and savoury sauce on the classic burger today”

The original sauce found on every Big Mac at Mcdonalds. Often described as creamy and savory. Originally created in 1968 by franchise owner Jim Delligatti in Pittsburgh. Coming in two flavours, which later got combined to form one. Many may refer to the Big Mac sauce as the "special sauce". This is because of a public commericial in 1974 which called it the "special sauce" gained popularity. Firmly establishing it as the "special sauce".

Ingredients

  • 1 Cup Mayonnaise
  • 1⁄2 Relish
  • 2 Tbps Brown mustard
  • 11⁄2 Tsp White vinegar
  • 11⁄4 Tsp Paprika
  • 1 Tsp Onion powder
  • 1 Tsp Granulated garlic
  • 1⁄4 Tsp Ground white pepper

Instructions

  1. Measure out all ingredients and combine in a large bowl.
  2. Wait 1hr for the mixture to rest before serving

Extra

Serve as either a burger topping or dipping sauce. Both work extremly well.
